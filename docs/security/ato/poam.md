# Plan of Action and Milestones

Every open finding in the package: checklist items (`checklists/summary.md`), partially implemented
controls (`ssp-narratives.md`) and image scan findings (`scans/`). Generated by `scripts/ato/poam` from
`poam.json`; `poam.csv` has the DoD POA&M template's columns for eMASS. Scheduled completion dates
are the product owner's to set: `[[DATE]]`. A site adds its own items.
